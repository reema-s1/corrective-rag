# Nimbus Companion App

The Nimbus Companion app (iOS 16+ and Android 11+) is used for firmware updates, EQ customization, and Find My Nimbus.

Firmware updates install automatically when the device is charging and connected to the app. To check manually, open the app, choose the device, then go to Settings > About > Check for Update.

Find My Nimbus shows the last known location of earbuds and watches and can play a sound on a connected device.
