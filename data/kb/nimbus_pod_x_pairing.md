# Nimbus Pod X — Pairing and Reset

To pair Nimbus Pod X earbuds with a new phone, place both earbuds in the charging case, open the lid, and hold the case button for 5 seconds until the status light flashes white. The earbuds then appear as "Nimbus Pod X" in the phone's Bluetooth menu.

To factory reset, hold the case button for 15 seconds until the light flashes amber three times. A reset erases all paired devices and restores the default EQ profile.

Pod X can remember up to 8 paired devices but connects to only 2 at a time (multipoint).
