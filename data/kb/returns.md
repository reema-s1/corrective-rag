# Returns and Refunds

Products bought directly from the Nimbus online store can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they are undamaged and include all accessories. Opened earbuds are accepted for return but must be sanitized by Nimbus before resale.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after the return is inspected. Products bought from third-party retailers must be returned to that retailer.
