# Nimbus Watch 2 — Battery and Charging

Nimbus Watch 2 lasts up to 4 days in normal use and up to 14 days in Power Saver mode, which disables the always-on display and continuous heart-rate tracking.

Charging from 0 to 100% takes about 75 minutes on the included magnetic puck charger. Third-party Qi chargers are not supported.

If the watch will not charge, clean the four gold contacts on the back with a dry cloth and confirm the puck's LED is solid green.
