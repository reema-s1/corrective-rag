# Troubleshooting — Audio Cuts Out or One Earbud Is Silent

If audio cuts out on Nimbus Pod X, first make sure the earbud firmware is up to date in the Nimbus Companion app. Keep the phone within 10 meters and avoid body blocking, for example a phone in a back pocket.

If only one earbud plays sound, put both earbuds in the case for 30 seconds, then take them out again. If the problem continues, factory reset the earbuds and pair them again.
