# Nimbus Pod X — Specifications

Battery life: 7 hours of playback per charge with noise cancellation on, 9 hours with it off. The charging case provides 3 additional full charges, for 28 hours total.

A 10-minute quick charge gives about 90 minutes of playback. The case charges over USB-C or Qi wireless charging.

Water resistance rating: IPX5 (sweat and splash resistant, not submersible). Pod X supports Bluetooth 5.3 with LE Audio and the LC3 codec.
