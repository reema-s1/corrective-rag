# Nimbus Limited Warranty

All Nimbus hardware comes with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, such as drops, cracked screens, and liquid damage beyond the rated resistance, is not covered.

NimbusCare+ extends coverage to 3 years and adds up to two accidental-damage repairs per year, each with a $49 service fee. NimbusCare+ must be purchased within 60 days of the device purchase.

Proof of purchase is required for all warranty claims.
